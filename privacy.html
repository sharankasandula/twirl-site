Twirl privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Twirl is a countdown timer published by STHIRAA HEALTHCARE SERVICES LLP. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • Twirl has no internet access. It never sends anything off your phone.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The only thing it saves is your current timer and the last duration you used, kept on your phone so the timer survives a restart. If you turn on Android backup, that small setting may be included in your own Google backup.

Permissions it uses: notifications (to show the countdown and ring when time is up), exact alarms (to ring on time), vibration, and "run at startup" (to bring back a running timer after a reboot).
